Mechanical Engineering Faculty Information

 

 

The School of Mechanical Engineering at Yonsei University is planning to hire numerous tenure-track faculty members over the coming years. This initiative reflects our commitment to academic excellence and our intent to bolster research and education in various sub-disciplines of mechanical engineering. Our goal is to expand our faculty base to enhance our research capabilities and academic offerings. We are seeking innovative thinkers and leaders who are eager to contribute to our dynamic and collaborative environment. Prospective candidates are encouraged to submit their curriculum vitae in pdf format as a preliminary step in joining our community. For questions or further information, please contact the Faculty Search Committee at mech@yonsei.ac.kr

We are seeking talented faculty candidates across the full spectrum of mechanical engineering disciplines. Regardless of your specialization, if you possess a passion for teaching and a record of research excellence, we encourage you to express your interest.

Ideal candidates will hold a Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering or a related field and have a proven track record of excellence in research and teaching. Sepecifically, the areas of research fields of our major interest include following keywords (but not limited to). We value candidates who can bring diverse perspectives and interdisciplinary approaches to address complex engineering challenges.

 

    ■ Mechanics, Materials
    ■ Thermal/Fluid Systems
    ■ Dynamics systems and Control
    ■ Manufacturing and Design
    ■ Emerging Areas including Bio/Medical/Nano/Micro/Optical Technology
